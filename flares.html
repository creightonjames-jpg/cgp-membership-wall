How it works

Five blooms along the floor, each breathing on its own clock between 3.4 and 5.1 seconds, started mid cycle so they never pulse in unison.

Gold core falling off through ember orange into scarlet, laid on in screen blend so it reads as light rather than paint on the picture.

Opacity and transform only, no blur filters, so it stays cheap on an older phone.

Anyone who asked their phone for less motion gets a lit floor with no pulsing.
